An engineer who reads the room and the datasheet.

I'm drawn to the parts of engineering that sit between hardware and people — designing a system is one thing, but getting a team to build it under pressure, on a deadline, is the part I find most rewarding.

I hope to use what I'm learning at UCT to give back to the country I call home. I like learning new things, testing what I can actually do under pressure, and I have a genuine soft spot for anything with a plug, a processor, or a trail map.
