Op-Amp Audio Circuit

A hand-wired stripboard circuit built around op-amp ICs, prototyped and bench-tested driving a speaker.

What it was

An analog circuit built by hand on stripboard, wiring three 8-pin op-amp ICs and a trimmer potentiometer to shape and drive an audio signal into a speaker. I built it stage by stage, checking each section on the oscilloscope and bench power supply before wiring in the next, so a fault in one stage never got buried under the next.

  • Hand-wired the circuit on stripboard around three op-amp stages and a trimmer pot.
  • Verified each stage on the oscilloscope and bench power supply before adding the next.
  • Drove a speaker directly from the finished circuit to confirm the output.
