Lead Compensator Circuit

An op-amp lead compensator, hand-built on veroboard, designed to control the altitude of a simulated quadcopter.

More about it

For EEE3094S (Control Systems Engineering), I designed and built an analog circuit to control the altitude of a simulated quadcopter rig ("KittiCopter"). This project was an extension of a proportional controller from an earlier lab, redesigned to ensure the system met the settling-time and overshoot targets.

  • Designed the compensator using root-locus analysis to meet my individual system requirements.
  • Used Matlab to simulate the system to ensure the settling-time and overshoot targets were met with my design.
  • Converted the transfer function into resistor and capacitor values and designed the op-amp circuit.
  • Soldered the circuit onto veroboard, continuity checking as I went to ensure no short circuits.
  • Added a small filtering resistor after testing picked up noise on the input signal.
  • Connected the circuit to the KittiCopter simulator - it settled faster than required and met the overshoot requirement.
