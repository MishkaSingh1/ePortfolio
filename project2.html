USB-C Power Board

A populated PCB built around a USB-C connector — designed, assembled, and bench-tested against a reference board.

What it was

A custom power board centred on a USB-C input, populated with SMD regulators, protection components, and a header for programming/debug access. Once assembled, I brought it up on the bench — powering it from a reference programmer board and probing test points with a multimeter to verify the rail voltages before trusting it with anything downstream.

  • Assembled the board — SMD ICs, passives, and the USB-C connector.
  • Brought it up on the bench, powered from a reference programmer board.
  • Probed test points with a multimeter to verify voltage rails before further testing.
